# scorbot/ (public SDK)

Facade and pure helpers over the legacy USB code. Read the root [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md) invariants first. `import scorbot` must never import `usb` or touch `sys.path` (`tests/test_simulated.py:test_import_loads_no_usb_and_leaves_sys_path_alone`); `robot.py` imports `usb` only inside `connect` and `disconnect`.

## Modules

| File | Contract |
|---|---|
| `robot.py:Scorbot` | Only class that sends commands. Two daemon threads (sync, command) run the legacy loops; queues `_commands`, `_results`, `_sync`, `_reads`. Command codes it sends: 16 disable, 17 enable, 18 home, 4-13 jogs, 14/15 gripper (only from `move_gripper`), 21 stream, 528 exit. Never 19. `request_stop()` sets `_stop_event` (no lock, callable from another thread); the legacy jog loop then ends early with result 14 and `jog_joint` raises `MotionStopped` after `_settled_after_stop`. `get_state_and_packet()` also returns the raw reply bytes. `start_stream()` queues order 21 with the stream's `source` callable and returns a `Stream`; `_end_stream` collects the worker's answer. While `_stream` is set every other command is refused before it logs or queues anything |
| `limits.py` | The limits several modules must agree on: `ARM_MOTORS`, `RECORDED_MOTORS`, `MAX_JOG_DEG` (5), `TRAVEL_CAP_DEG` (180 since 2026-10-06: past every joint's range, so the joint limits bind), `DRIFT_COUNTS` (20), `STABLE_COUNTS` (2). Pure, imports nothing from `scorbot`. Change a limit here and nowhere else; the other modules' names (`follow.TRAVEL_CAP_DEG`, `Scorbot.STREAM_TRAVEL_CAP_MAX_DEG`, `lab.session.DRIFT_COUNTS`...) are aliases, and `tests/test_limits.py` fails if one stops being so. In the motion fingerprint. Raising one needs lab evidence (the owner lifted `TRAVEL_CAP_DEG` without it on 2026-10-06; see `docs/project/PROJECT_LOG.md`) |
| `source_model.py` | The second calibration tier: joint angles (degrees, the USNA toolboxes' BSEPR convention) from motor counts and back, using the vendor formula in `vendor_model.py`. `angles_from_counts`, `counts_from_angles`, `arm_counts_from_angles` (what a stream is given), `pose_for_arm` (the pose with the wrist motors left alone: the relative pitch changes as shoulder and elbow move, because the wrist motors hold the gripper's angle to the floor), `LIMITS_DEG` and `outside_limits` (the narrower of the toolbox's and the vendor's joint limits), `limit_window` (those limits in counts, for base and shoulder), `motion_window` and `outside_window` (limits and travel cap together; wrist must stay at home), `xyzpr_from_angles` and `angles_from_xyzpr` (tool point and back, elbow-up, with the vendor's dimensions; these load `kinematics.py` and NumPy on first use, never at import). `Scorbot.jog_joint` and `start_stream` use `limit_window`: the shoulder has under 4 degrees of travel above home. They also check the whole pose (`limit_excess`, `pose_limit_excess`, `pose_limit_problem`, added 2026-10-06 when the 10 degree cap was lifted): a joint's angle can depend on other motors' counts (the elbow follows the shoulder; the wrist pitch follows both while the wrist motors stand still), so the elbow's own limit and the pitch limit are checked on all counts at once. `home_window` gives each motor's edge alone from home, for sliders and messages only; an arm already past a limit may be jogged back in, never further out. `STATUS` says "from sources ... not measured"; keep it on anything shown to a user. Assumes the legacy home is the vendor's home pose; the acceptance run checks that. Pure, no USB |
| `joint_move.py` | Coupled joint moves: `vector(joint)` is the motor counts per degree when only one joint's angle changes (from the vendor formulas through `source_model`: shoulder 1 degree = shoulder +113.5, elbow -113.5, wrist pair 27.85 as a pure pitch), `CoupledTarget` is the cumulative target of one run (pre-home phase, homing search, park) with the counts compared by `signed_count_delta`, `proportional_jogs` keeps the motors in step on a long move. Pure. Keep one `CoupledTarget` for a whole phase: a fresh one per move loses what the coupled motors are owed and drifts (a test pins this). `Scorbot._coupled_run` / `_coupled_jog` execute it with legacy jogs, including the wrist pitch jog (orders 10 and 11) and never the roll. In the motion fingerprint. Never run on the arm |
| `inch_home.py` | Home by inching: `inch_to_edge(read_on, move, ...)` walks one joint to its switch's near edge with small steps (toward the switch up to a cap, back and the other way if not found, then off the switch and back to its edge). Pure; constants from the vendor homing trace (`ORDER`, `APPROACH_COUNTS`, `OFFSET_COUNTS`). `Scorbot.home_inch` supplies the jogs (`_jog_joint(..., homing=True)`) and the switch reads, then moves the vendor offset and records the counts as the session home. In the motion fingerprint. Unverified on the arm |
| `follow.py` | Two followers with one interface (`observe`, `step_toward`), on counts from the session home. `TargetFollower`: at most one 1 degree jog per call, waits for it (replay of a keyboard recording). `StreamFollower`: each call moves the target of `start_stream` and returns at once (a policy loop); `close()` ends the stream. Both refuse wrist targets and anything past the travel cap with `FollowRefused`. Used by the simulator-only LeRobot plugin |
| `packet.py:TrackedInputEndpoint` | Wraps the USB IN endpoint; copies each response out of the legacy's reused buffer, numbers it, timestamps it. `snapshot(after_index=, max_age=)` returns only a fresh, full-length packet or raises `TimeoutError` |
| `state.py` | `decode_state` (raises on short packet or sign byte not 127/128), `RobotState`, offsets `ENCODER_OFFSETS`, `HOME_SWITCH_BITS` (polarity unverified) |
| `calibration.py` | `signed_count_delta`, `load_calibration` (base/shoulder/elbow only) |
| `nominal.py` | Manual values, stdlib only. Enforces span, never signed limits |
| `simulated.py` | `SimulatedScorbot` overrides `connect`, `disconnect`, `get_state_and_packet` (which `get_state` goes through), `_record` only. `SimulatedController.inject(kind)` arms one-shot faults from `FAULT_KINDS` |
| `preflight.py` | `python -m scorbot.preflight`: enumerate only, never open, reset or configure |
| `kinematics.py` | Offline, unvalidated DH model. Wired into no motion command |
| `arm_chain.py` | `link_poses(q)`: 4x4 pose of every link for five joint angles, and `skeleton(q)`, the arm as a line. For viewers only; wired into no motion command. Frames follow the community ER-4U CAD model (x forward, z up, arm level along +x at zero, positive lifts) so its meshes can be placed with fixed offsets; this is not `kinematics.py`'s convention (wrist pitch differs by 90 degrees, checked in `tests/test_arm_chain.py`). Three geometries, `MESH_MODEL` (default), `MANUAL` and `VENDOR_INI`, which disagree on the shoulder axis (346, 364 and 349 mm up; 29, 16 and 16 mm forward); none is measured. Unvalidated |
| `arm_view.py` | `python -m scorbot.arm_view --simulate` or `--pose`: a live 3D picture in Rerun (`viz` extra, imported only when a view opens). Pure part: `chain_angles_from_counts` (vendor formula and toolbox signs, a prior), `scene`, `visual_offset`, `find_meshes`. `ArmView` is the only Rerun call site. Link meshes are optional and never committed (`models/er4u_meshes/README.md`); without them the arm is a line. Simulator only for now; reads state, commands nothing. Every view carries the UNVALIDATED notice: keep it |
| `streaming.py` | `StreamCore`: the streaming logic with no USB, threads or sleeps (targets in, one jerk-limited step per period out, travel cap, per-motor `window`, the whole-pose `pose_check` on every target and every setpoint, lead limit, stop, hold, faults). `Stream`: the adapter the worker calls once per period. Ruckig imported lazily (`planning` extra). In the motion fingerprint. Never run on the arm; requirements in `docs/specs/2026-10-04-streaming-driver-requirements.md` |
| `vendor_profile.py` | The vendor's jerk-limited motion profile (time, acceleration fraction, jerk fraction) and `Speed` mapping, read from `USBC.dll` (`docs/protocol/VENDOR_DLL_PROTOCOL.md` section 11). Pure, stdlib only. `streaming.prior_limits` takes a stream's starting acceleration and jerk from its velocity-jog time fractions, so it is in the motion fingerprint. A prior from disassembly, not measured |
| `vendor_model.py` | The vendor's count/angle conversion and wrist mixing, read from `USBC.dll` (`docs/protocol/VENDOR_DLL_PROTOCOL.md` section 10). Pure, stdlib only. `source_model.py` wraps it for the SDK; nothing else should call it directly. A prior, never calibration: do not feed it to `calibration.py` or use it to enable wrist jogs |
| `lab/` | Guided session. `session.py` LabSession engine (no print/input; Operator protocol), `terminal.py` front end, `profile.py` lab.json, `review.py` shared with `scripts/review_lab_logs.py --session`, `teleop.py` teleop mode and episodes (one press = one step, release gate), `camera.py` session camera with liveness. `--inch-home` (`LabSession(inch_home=True)`) runs the vendor routine: an optional pre-home step (typed `SHOULDER +1`-style moves through `Scorbot.pre_home_jog`, one motor each by default and the coupled motors too with `--coupled-pre-home`, motors enabled first), `Scorbot.home_inch` instead of the legacy search with the same typed words, and a "return home before motors off" prompt after the keys (`Scorbot.park_at_home`). The default flow has none of the three. Adds no motion capability: 1 degree steps, base/shoulder/elbow, net travel cap `limits.TRAVEL_CAP_DEG` (180) and, through `jog_joint`, the whole-pose joint limits |
| `provenance.py` | SHA-256 over `_SOURCE_FILES` (motion path). Add a file there if it can change what is sent. `calibration.py` is in it because `signed_count_delta` shapes a stream's setpoints |
| `transport/codec.py` | Pure OUT-packet codec, stdlib only (never `usb`, `openScorbot`, `numpy`). Golden tests prove it byte-identical to the legacy code; not used by `Scorbot` yet (USB upgrade phase A). Unverified against the controller |

## scorbot/session/

- `record.py:SessionWriter._emit` is the single write path: validates required keys (`schemas.REQUIRED`), strict JSON, splices a `_rec` block (seq, logged/observed monotonic ns), flushes per message. A write failure sets `_broken`; later calls raise `SessionError` and `close()` does not append a summary.
- `create()` uses `open(..., "xb")`: sessions are never overwritten. `data_source` is `real`, `simulated` or `synthetic`; `log_state` rejects a state whose `simulated` flag disagrees.
- `BestEffortRecorder` swallows recorder errors after the first warning. Lab scripts write JSONL first, then call the recorder.
- `replay.py:load_session` streams records, so crashed sessions load; damaged tail is a warning, earlier corruption an error. `analysis.py` is pure (no printing); its count deltas use `signed_count_delta`, matching `scripts/review_lab_logs.py`.
- `rerun_view.py`: `session_items` is pure (no Rerun import) and is the unit to test; `send`/`view` are the only Rerun call sites. Keep `rerun` imports inside functions; the `viz` extra is optional and not in `dev`.
- Adding a topic or field: edit `schemas.py` (`TOPICS`, `REQUIRED`, `PROPERTIES`), keep properties permissive (`additionalProperties` true) so old sessions load, and cover it in `tests/test_schemas.py` and `tests/test_session.py`.

## Rules

- Gates live only in `Scorbot`. Add a check to the facade, not to a script; the simulator then exercises it for free. If you override a method in `SimulatedScorbot`, mirror the real cleanup and fault behavior (its `connect` sets `_fault` directly instead of calling `_latch_fault`).
- Lock nesting: `_motion_lock` (RLock, motion methods) outside, then `_lock` (in `_command`) or `_state_lock` (in `get_state`). Never take `_motion_lock` while holding the others. `_command` on error queues a best-effort `[16, 1, 1]` and then raises; it skips the disable when the worker crashed (`_WorkerCrashed`) because nobody would answer.
- A stop is not a fault: `MotionStopped` (a `ScorbotError`) leaves the session homed and enabled, and `_command` re-raises it without latching. It becomes a fault only if the arm does not settle (`stop_settle_failed`). `MotionStopped.started` is False when a pending request refused the jog before anything was sent; a request that arrives while the move is closing is logged as `stop_too_late` and the jog completes. A caller that catches `ScorbotError` must not treat `MotionStopped` as a dead session. `_stop_event` is not `_cancel_event`: the first ends one jog, the second means the session is dying.
- Streaming: targets are motor counts from this session's home for base, shoulder and elbow only. `StreamCore.step` never raises and `Stream.source` catches everything, because an exception there would kill the worker thread mid-stream; a problem becomes `core.fault` and a stop, and `Stream.source` reports it through `Scorbot._stream_faulted` on the worker (latch, queued disable, one `stream_fault` row), so the session is faulted even if the caller never ends the stream; `_end_stream` then only collects the result. A failure `_end_stream` finds itself (a timeout) is pushed into the core with `core.fail`, so a worker that comes back stops. `Stream.source` also paces steps to `period_s` (the only sleep in the stream path outside the legacy loop). The lead limit is checked on the setpoint about to be sent. `start_stream` bounds `period_s` and `hold_timeout_s` (`streaming.PERIOD_RANGE_S`, `HOLD_TIMEOUT_RANGE_S`): a long period makes each step a jump, a long hold timeout switches the hold off. A session fault latched on another thread stops a running stream at its next step. `_end_stream` latches before it writes the (large) `stream_trace` row, and treats Ctrl-C while it waits like `_command` does: fault, cancel, disable, then re-raise. The emergency bit (`use_emergency_bit`) is implemented but off by default until the lab confirms that byte (lab plan V6). Pacing on the echoed message number is not built: a first attempt could never be fed and was removed on 2026-10-05; it waits for lab plan V4. Raising `STREAM_TRAVEL_CAP_MAX_DEG` needs lab evidence and a project-log entry.
- Gripper: `move_gripper("open"|"close")` (and `open_gripper`, `close_gripper`) sends legacy order 14 or 15 and returns a `GripperMove` (planned and moved counts, `full_travel`). Needs motors enabled, not homing; refused while a stream is active. Stopping short is a result, not a fault: an object and the end of travel look the same in counts. Before sending anything it waits for the arm to be at rest (`_settled_after_stop`), because a jog can return inside its settle band. It faults if the arm is not at rest, if the legacy loop reports the gripper still moving (result 2), if any arm motor reads more than `GRIPPER_ARM_TOLERANCE_COUNTS` different afterwards, or if the gripper count moved the wrong way for the direction asked (`gripper_fault`): which legacy order opens is inherited, not measured. `request_stop` ends it early with `MotionStopped`. The planned travel comes from `motion_profile.gripper_increments` and the legacy `pinza` settings. Never run on the arm; the vendor closes its gripper another way (`docs/protocol/VENDOR_DLL_PROTOCOL.md` section 12).
- After any fault `connect()` refuses; callers build a new instance. `disconnect()` keeps the USB handle open if a worker is still running and raises. Do not "fix" that by force-closing.
- `enabled` and `homed` are command history, not controller truth (`enabled=None` after a fault). Do not present them as measured. The MOTORS LED is the only independent evidence.
- `home()` sets `_home_counts`; `jog_joint` and `get_joint_angles` derive angles as `home_angle + signed_count_delta(count, session_home) / counts_per_degree`. A calibration `home_count` is only checked at home (`validate_home`), not used for angles.
- `move_joint` = one jog to a soft-limit-checked target; raises if the step exceeds `max_jog_degrees`; latches a fault if it lands more than 2 degrees off.
- `get_state` errors inside motion paths become a latched fault through `_motion_state`. Use `_motion_state` in new motion code, not `get_state`.
- `signed_encoder_counts` jumps by 65536 at the 0/65535 seam. Use `signed_count_delta` on `encoder_counts`. `signed_count_delta` uses modulus 65535 and raises on 32767/32768 (ambiguous).
- Log rows (`_record`) are consumed by `scripts/watch_lab_log.py` and `scripts/review_lab_logs.py`. Renaming an event name silently breaks alarms; update `ALARM_EVENTS`.
- `decode_state` needs 49 bytes (`PACKET_MIN_LENGTH`); the simulator emits 64. Real packet length beyond 49 is not characterised here.
- Calibration loader requires `status == "validated"`, matching `robot_id`, 64-hex `source_sha256`, minimum point counts, errors <= 2 degrees, span within manual travel. Keep `scripts/fit_calibration.py:fit` producing exactly what the loader accepts.

## Gotchas

- `Scorbot._legacy(name)` inserts `openScorbot/` at the front of `sys.path` and imports by bare name. `Scorbot().preview_jog` and `_legacy("libdef")` therefore import the legacy modules (and `libdef` imports `usb.core`, PyUSB needed) but do not open the device.
- `connect()` calls `conf.setup()`, which creates `openScorbot/data.json` (git-ignored) once. Legacy modules read it at import.
- `SimulatedController.step_delay_s` defaults to 0; a simulated jog is instant and exact. It says nothing about timing, dynamics, backlash or real controller errors. Never use it to claim protocol or physical correctness.
- Faults injected via `stale_feedback` raise `TimeoutError` in `snapshot`; `worker_crash` sleeps 0.3 s to mimic a still-alive thread.

## Testing changes

`python -m unittest tests.test_python_api tests.test_arm_control tests.test_simulated -v`, then the full suite. New gate: add a test that asserts nothing was queued (`robot._commands.empty()` or `robot.sim.commands` unchanged) when the gate rejects. New fault path: add its kind to `FAULT_KINDS` and cover it in `test_every_fault_kind_latches_the_session`.

## Do not

- Do not add absolute or Cartesian motion or wrist jogs without measured evidence and a matching test flip. The one exception is `mover.py`, simulator only (owner, 2026-10-05): it turns degree and millimetre targets into count targets inside the travel cap and refuses a robot that is not a `SimulatedScorbot`. Do not loosen that check. The gripper has one path, `move_gripper`; do not add a second, a position target or a force setting without lab evidence.
- Do not raise the 5 degree ceiling or widen speed 1-20.
- Do not import `openScorbot` modules at top level of `scorbot/*` (breaks import without PyUSB).
- Do not report facts through `print`; log via `_record`. The stderr banner in `_worker_died` is deliberate.
